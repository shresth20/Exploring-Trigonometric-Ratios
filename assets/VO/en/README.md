# Voice-over clips

One Opus-in-WebM clip per voiced key, named `<key>.webm` -- the keys and
their lines are in `locales/locales.json` (the `voiceOver.en.files` map).
Drop the recordings in here and they play; until then each line is typed
at the Typer's own pace and the beats hold their time.

Word cues (`voiceOver.en.cues`, ms from the clip's start, one per word)
make a line type word for word as it is heard.
